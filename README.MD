## The Game

Players spawn in the hell with armoured car because they deserve it. There's also other AI armoured vehicle.
There's path to upper floor.
In order to escape from the hell you have to find the path to reach the upper floor.
There will be many floors with many tastes.
when you try to leave the current floor the other AI vehicle comes at you to prevent you from going up.
You will face many kind of AI vehicle in different floors. One might rush at you with the speed of the light to throw you out from the current floor.
You have guns to kill them or push them or simply run(it works sometimes.)
after overcoming all the obstacle you see heaven where you are not meant to be but the heaven welcomes you
